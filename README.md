🍪 The "Savoury Nutty" Recipe (10 Biscuits)
•	Base: Roasted Chickpea Flour + Milled Oats.
•	Fats: Sunflower Butter (No Xylitol).
•	Umami: Nutritional Yeast.
•	Binder: Blackstrap Molasses.
•	Nutrient Core: Specific nutrient premix.
________________________________________
🛠️ Production & Safety
1.	Cold-Process: Mix ingredients. Do NOT heat above 45°C (113°F) to protect nutrients.
2.	Dehydration: Dry until "snap-dry" in a solar or low-temp dehydrator.
3.	Safety: Monitor for rancidity (fishy smell).
